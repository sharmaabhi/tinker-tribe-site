Why Tinker Tribe

Most courses go deep. We get you started.

Long courses are great once you know what you love. But how do you find out what you love? You try lots of interesting things, quickly. That’s what we’re for.

How it works

From “huh, interesting” to “I made this”.

  1. 1

    Discover

    Pick any topic in the free library and read a short section. No account, no fee.

  2. 2

    Try

    Do the activity: spot a bird, try a maths shortcut, wire an LED, find a planet tonight.

  3. 3

    Build

    When something clicks, join a 3-day weekend workshop and build a real project to take home.

  4. 4

    Share

    Show it off on showcase day, then team up with other kids for the next idea.

Topics

Interesting things, explained simply.

Start wherever your curiosity points. Each topic begins with the everyday questions (why does a plane fly? which bird is that?) and gets hands-on fast.

Robotics

Build a small moving, reacting robot from motors, sensors and a microcontroller — no prior electronics needed.

Electronics

LEDs, circuits, simple sensors — the building blocks that make everything else on this list possible.

Astronomy

Stargazing nights, spotting constellations and planets, and the kind of space facts that don't fit in a textbook.

Code & AI

Teach a program — or a robot — to respond to you. Block-based or simple Python, depending on age.

Quantum Curiosity

Atoms, light and the weird rules of the very small — explained with experiments and stories, not equations.

Weekend workshops

When the spark catches, build something real.

Every workshop runs the same arc, whatever the topic — three days, one project, one showcase.

Day 1 — Spark

A demo, a "why this is cool" moment, and a small guided build so everyone leaves day one with something working.

Day 2 — Build

The real project. Guided, but with real choices along the way — this is where it stops feeling like a kit and starts feeling like theirs.

Day 3 — Showcase

Kids present what they built. Parents are invited. This is also usually everyone's favourite part.
