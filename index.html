Not another tuition class.

School covers the syllabus. Tinker Tribe covers everything it doesn't have time for — the hobby-level, hands-on version of robotics, electronics, astronomy and code. No exams, no professional-depth jargon. Just enough to build something, break something, and want to build the next thing.

How it works

  1. 1

    Discover

    Browse short, visual explainers on the website or follow along on Instagram — no sign-up needed to start getting curious.

  2. 2

    Build

    Join a 3-day weekend workshop. Small batches, real tools, a project you take home at the end.

  3. 3

    Showcase

    Demo what you built to parents and the group — and if you want to go further, find a team for your next project.

Robotics

The weekend format

Every workshop runs the same arc, whatever the topic — three days, one project, one showcase.

Day 1 — Spark

A demo, a "why this is cool" moment, and a small guided build so everyone leaves day one with something working.

Day 2 — Build

The real project. Guided, but with real choices along the way — this is where it stops feeling like a kit and starts feeling like theirs.

Day 3 — Showcase

Kids present what they built. Parents are invited. This is also usually everyone's favourite part.
